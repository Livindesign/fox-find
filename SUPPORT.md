# Support Fox Find / 支持 Fox Find

## 中文

Fox Find 免费使用，源码采用 MIT 许可。赞助完全自愿，用于持续维护、
兼容性测试和文档完善；不影响功能使用。

**我们已选择爱发电作为赞助平台，创作者主页仍在准备，当前没有可用的收款链接。**
主页确认后，会统一显示在这里、GitHub Sponsor 按钮和应用的「关于」页面。

爱发电的赞助方案按月计价。只想支持一次时，选择 **1 个月** 即可；
[平台官方说明](https://guide.afdian.com/faq/faq-for-users)表示没有自动续费或自动扣款。
它仍是按月计期的方案，不是独立的一次性打赏设置，支付前请确认所选月份与总金额。

赞助支持 Livindesign 对 Fox Find 的独立维护。赞助不附带专属功能、交付日期或优先支持承诺，
也不改变 MIT 许可。平台的支付及退款规则适用。

也欢迎通过可复现的问题报告、翻译、兼容性测试或分享使用体验支持项目。
感谢为 Fox Find 提供基础的 [Oil Find](https://github.com/oil-oil/oil-find)、
[DigUp](https://github.com/ARahim3/DigUp) 和 [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)；
你也可以直接支持它们。来源与许可见 [NOTICE](NOTICE.md)。

## English

Fox Find is free to use, with MIT-licensed source. Voluntary sponsorship will fund
maintenance, compatibility testing and documentation. All features remain available
without payment, subject to their technical requirements.

**Afdian is our chosen platform. The creator page is being prepared; no verified
payment link is enabled yet.** Once confirmed, the same link will appear here,
on the GitHub Sponsor button and in the app's About window.

Afdian plans are priced by month. To support once, select **one month**; its
[official FAQ](https://guide.afdian.com/faq/faq-for-users) states that there is no
automatic renewal or debit. This remains a monthly-duration plan, rather than a
separate one-time tip setting. Check the selected duration and total at checkout.

Sponsorship supports Livindesign's independent maintenance of Fox Find. It does not
promise exclusive features, delivery dates or priority support, and does not change
the MIT license. The platform's payment and refund rules apply.

You can also help with reproducible bug reports, translations, compatibility testing
or a short demo. We thank Oil Find, DigUp and llama.cpp for their foundations and
welcome support for their maintainers too. See [origins and acknowledgments](NOTICE.md).
